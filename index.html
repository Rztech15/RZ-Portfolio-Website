About

Mathematics Is How I Think and Technology Is How I Build

I am a Mathematician at Bahauddin Zakariya University who turns raw data into clear insight and ideas into working software. I lead Future Builders, a group working on AI, code and software projects, and I run RZ Commerce, my own online store.

Mathematics trains me to break a problem into assumptions, structure and proof. I apply the same habit to code: models like linear regression and gradient descent become programs I can test, and messy datasets become dashboards people can act on.

My research on the financial data of Pakistan's top companies was presented at FICPFSIT-2026. I am open to internships, freelance work and collaboration.
